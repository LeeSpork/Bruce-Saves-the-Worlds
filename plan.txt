I was gonna go to bed, but then Anneth/Fenna posted their playthrough! (https://youtu.be/vno4wod7Mws). This was my first time watching someone other than myself play the mod. This has given me too much motivation sleep is cancelled.

Oversights I've now noticed:
- the South pole draws big attention when you first spawn in. This is not ideal because there is nothing to do there until 7 minutes into the loop.
- the North pole does not draw any attention from afar. Bad! I wanted you to meet Corey before Bruce tells you to. Him telling you about her is intended to be a last resort to prevent players who failed to explore from getting stuck.
- You can ask Corey to send the launch signal before learning that Corey can communiate between planets. (Eh I guess it is obvious anyway now that I am adding satellite dishes as we speak)
